README.md Overview

Hello, I'm Murmur. Welcome to my blog!

欢 迎 来 看 我 的 blog ~~Welcome to my blog

I am currently focused on developing more efficient CUDA kernels and optimizing them at the PTX level. I also leverage existing compiler frameworks to register custom operators, enabling optimized model deployment. Building on my background in high-performance computing, I am also exploring the development of communication code for distributed training.

I’ll be sharing my latest insights and technical posts here from time to time. I’m always glad when other learners find my work helpful—after all, I’ve already stumbled into more than a few pitfalls myself, which is honestly pretty funny in hindsight. I also hope that more experienced developers will stop by and offer guidance on my learning journey. I have an insatiable thirst for knowledge. That would make me genuinely happy.

I am also very concerned about the impact of AI on society and have been following the latest discussions on PhilPapers. Personally, I believe that, despite the wide-ranging discussions, there has not yet been a genuine analysis of interpretability—a topic that requires a multidisciplinary approach and complementary development across fields. Gaps in related disciplines can be addressed through new insights gained from the analysis of AI, rather than merely using AI; AI itself also requires prior knowledge from other disciplines. This is a nearly impossible task, but I still hope to make some contribution in this field.
