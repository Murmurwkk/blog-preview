Navigation & Statistics

导航文章导读与术语表Navigation reading guide and glossary

The navigation posts examine shared uncertainty, trajectory geometry, radio evidence and the lifetime of corrections. The order below builds these ideas from the state representation to the decision to keep or retire a correction. Each post also explains its own experiment.

导航系列讨论共同不确定性、轨迹几何、无线观测和修正的生命周期。下面的顺序从状态表示讲到保留或撤销修正的决策；每篇文章也独立说明自身的实验。

建议阅读顺序Suggested reading order

  1. How Ignoring Cross-Covariance Corrupts a Navigation Velocity Estimate

    Begin with the joint state. A position observation also changes velocity and attitude through cross-covariance; this post shows what is lost when relative motion edges are treated as independent.先理解联合状态。位置观测会通过交叉协方差影响速度和姿态；这篇文章解释把相对运动边当成独立量时会丢失什么。

  2. Why a First-Order SE(2) Correction Stretches a Trajectory

    Then examine geometry. A large first-order rotation can stretch a trajectory, so a smaller residual does not by itself establish that a correction is valid.再看几何。大幅一阶旋转可能拉伸轨迹，因此残差变小本身不能证明修正合理。

  3. Replacing Gaussian Noise with Student-t in Robust WiFi Positioning

    Next examine the observation model. Separate the Student-t scale matrix from covariance, and distinguish a heavier tail from a different central curvature.接着看观测模型。区分 Student-t 的尺度矩阵与协方差，也区分重尾和中心曲率的变化。

  4. Evaluating Navigation Corrections on Held-Out Data with Shared Errors

    Use those ingredients to evaluate a candidate. Build with A and B, freeze the candidate and the scoring rule, and predict C while retaining shared uncertainty and the information available at each cutoff.然后评估候选修正。用 A、B 构造候选，冻结候选和评分规则，在保留共同不确定性及各时刻可用信息的条件下预测 C。

  5. When to Retire a Navigation Correction and How to Roll It Back

    Finally follow an accepted correction through time. A fallback branch must keep receiving legitimate later updates so that retiring a correction does not erase what happened afterwards.最后考察修正被接受之后的生命周期。回退分支需要持续接收合法的后续更新，撤销修正时才能保留后来发生的信息。

术语与记号Terms and labels

A/B/C stages · 验证阶段

A and B are observation segments used to estimate and check compatible candidate corrections, then to construct a joint candidate. C scores a future prediction after the candidate and its scoring rule are frozen. These labels describe roles in a protocol. A later time segment can still share inertial, map or device errors with earlier segments.

A、B 是用来分别估计、检查候选兼容性并构造联合候选的观测片段。冻结候选和评分规则后，再由 C 评价未来预测。这些字母表示协议中的角色；时间上分开仍可能共享惯性、地图或设备误差。

Snapshots A–D · 历史快照

In the geometry and Student-t studies, A–D identify saved, overlapping windows of one flat-floor walking log. They are not four independent trials, a time series of four consecutive updates, or the A/B/C validation stages. In the map study, A–D instead name four planned evaluation arms that vary the position labeling and field construction.

几何与 Student-t 实验中的 A–D 是同一条平地行走日志上保存的、互有重叠的窗口。它们不是四次独立试验，也不是四次连续更新组成的时间序列，更不是 A/B/C 验证阶段。地图研究中的 A–D 则是四个计划中的评估组，分别改变位置标注与场模型构建方式。

Motion · 惯性前端与相对运动约束

Motion names the learned inertial front end and the short-window relative motion constraints derived from its IMU input. A constraint carries uncertainty, and neighboring constraints can share uncertainty. A plotted trajectory is an estimate built from these inputs; its coordinates are not surveyed ground truth.

Motion 指学习式惯性前端，以及它根据 IMU 输入产生的短窗口相对运动约束。约束带有不确定性，相邻约束之间也可能存在共同不确定性。由这些输入得到的轨迹是估计结果，其坐标不是测绘真值。

Radio and Q · 无线位置假设与不确定性矩阵

Radio refers to WiFi-based position hypotheses. A provider may return several modes rather than one point. Q describes the uncertainty attached to a hypothesis; the observation model decides whether Q is used as a Gaussian covariance or a Student-t scale matrix. The two uses have different covariances.

Radio 指基于 WiFi 的位置假设，提供器可能返回多个模态而非一个点。Q 描述假设所带的不确定性；观测模型决定它用作高斯协方差还是 Student-t 尺度矩阵，两者对应的协方差不同。

POSI · 稀疏位置参考记录

POSI names the surveyed position reference records in the walking logs. In the covariance, geometry and Student-t comparisons, these sparse points evaluate the estimates rather than fit the correction. An error at the reference times does not measure the error of every intermediate state; each post states its interpolation and alignment rules.

POSI 是行走日志中的测绘位置参考记录。在共同协方差、几何和 Student-t 对比中，这些稀疏点用于评估估计结果，而非拟合修正。参考时刻的误差不能代表所有中间状态的误差；各篇分别说明插值和对齐规则。

T labels, sessions and Trials · 日志与采集分组

A label such as T31 is shorthand used in the posts. Session and device labels identify the particular recording, and each appendix records the exact input. The map study uses a Trial as a collection-session grouping for held-out evaluation. These identifiers are provenance labels, not performance scores. Reusing a log across posts does not create independent experimental replications; the same underlying errors can appear in several snapshots and analyses.

文中的 T31 这类编号是简写，具体记录还需结合 session 与设备标签，各篇附录给出准确输入。地图研究中的 Trial 是用于留出评估的采集会话分组。这些编号用于追溯来源，不是性能评分。同一日志出现在多篇文章中，不会因此变成多次独立实验；同一底层误差可能出现在多个快照和分析里。

EN, height and floor · 水平坐标、高度与楼层

EN denotes the east and north components in the local coordinate system. Height is a continuous vertical coordinate; floor is a discrete label. A floor can be correct while the estimated height drifts, and an improved horizontal fit can still damage vertical consistency.

EN 表示局部坐标系的东、北分量。高度是连续垂直坐标，楼层是离散标签。楼层正确时，估计高度仍可能漂移；水平拟合改善时，垂直一致性也可能变差。

KEEP, candidate and fallback · 保留、候选与回退分支

KEEP is the existing explanation used for comparison with a candidate correction. A fallback branch keeps receiving legitimate new observations while omitting the long-lived correction being tested. Returning to that branch preserves later information; restoring an old snapshot would discard it.

KEEP 是与候选修正比较时使用的现有解释。回退分支持续接收合法的新观测，同时不包含被考察的长期修正。切回这个分支能够保留后续信息，恢复旧快照则会丢弃这些信息。
